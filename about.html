About Me

        As a computer teacher, I am deeply passionate about technology and its power to transform the way we learn and live. 
        I enjoy exploring new software, programming languages, and digital tools that can make learning more interactive and 
        practical for students. Teaching computers allows me to share this excitement with my students and help them 
        understand how technology shapes our modern world. My main interest lies in making complex computer concepts easy 
        and enjoyable to understand. I love designing creative activities where students can apply what they learn — whether 
        it’s creating a simple web page, writing a short program, or using MS Office tools effectively. Watching my students 
        develop problem-solving skills and confidence in using technology gives me great satisfaction. I also have a keen 
        interest in staying updated with the latest trends in the tech world, such as artificial intelligence, web 
        development, and cybersecurity. By learning continuously, I aim to bring fresh and relevant knowledge to my classroom. 
        My goal as a teacher is not only to teach computer skills but also to inspire students to think critically and become 
        lifelong learners in the digital age.
